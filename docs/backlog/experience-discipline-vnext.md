## Acceptance Criteria

- Fettle owns one versioned shared experience contract and a coherent UX,
  Planning, UI, and UAT discipline bundle.
- Independent harm, scope, autonomy, and uncertainty triggers route proportional
  work without a blended risk score.
- High-consequence unknowns, uncontrolled side effects, missing recovery, known
  accessibility barriers, deceptive choices, authority mismatch, false evidence
  claims, and broken controlled-work traceability fail visibly.
- Behavioral evaluations detect both unsafe under-analysis and ritual
  over-processing without relying on keyword-only grading.
- Evidence scope, accessibility status, legal uncertainty, and human-review limits
  remain explicit.
- Host installation is previewable, opt-in, integrity-checked, non-destructive,
  idempotent, and reversible.
- A blinded held-out evaluation and advisory pilot meet pre-registered thresholds
  before any active global discipline is replaced.
